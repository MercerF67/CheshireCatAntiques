1910

The year the house at 301 East Avenue E was built for Benjamin F. Berkeley.

A shop that feels like a museum, because in a way it is one.

Before it was full of spurs and silver, our building was a home. Benjamin F. Berkeley, who served as Alpine's first mayor and later in the Texas Senate, lived here when the town was still young. We think he would like what has become of the place.

Cheshire Cat Antiques is run by one family, and each of us has a corner of expertise along with a long memory for the region's history. Come in to hunt for something specific, or just come in to talk. Plenty of visitors who meant to stop for a minute end up staying an hour.
